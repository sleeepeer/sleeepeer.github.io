## About Me

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Informatics at the **Pennsylvania State University**, fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Jinyuan Jia](https://jinyuan-jia.github.io/). I received my B.Eng in Computer Science from [Wuhan University](https://www.whu.edu.cn/) in 2024.

I am currently a Research Scientist Intern at [ByteDance](https://www.bytedance.com/), working on Doubao safety and security.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on developing secure and reliable LLMs and AI agents. I study both sides of the problem &mdash; the attacks and vulnerabilities, and the defenses, robustness, and alignment &mdash; with a particular focus on prompt injection.

- **Prompt Injection:** attacks, defenses, and red teaming for LLMs and AI agents
- **RAG Security:** knowledge corruption attacks and contextual attribution
- **Evaluation:** benchmarking LLMs and AI agents under realistic and comprehensive settings
- **Post-Training:** improving robustness through RL finetuning without degrading utility

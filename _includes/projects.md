## Open Source Projects

- **[Open-Prompt-Injection](https://github.com/liu00222/Open-Prompt-Injection)** &mdash; the standard benchmark for prompt injection attacks and defenses. Co-author.
- **[PoisonedRAG](https://github.com/sleeepeer/PoisonedRAG)** &mdash; the first knowledge corruption attack to retrieval-augmented generation. Lead contributor and co-first author.
- **[PIArena](https://github.com/sleeepeer/PIArena)** &mdash; a dynamic evaluation platform for prompt injection attacks and defenses. Lead contributor and first author.
- **[PISanitizer](https://github.com/sleeepeer/PISanitizer)** &mdash; prompt sanitization defense for long-context LLMs. Lead contributor and first author.
